Privacy Policy

Flight Landing 3D for iOS and Android · Varala Studios

Effective date: 15 November 2026

In short: Flight Landing 3D does not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal information. The game has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. Your progress stays on your device.

1. Who we are

Flight Landing 3D ("the game") is published by Varala Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the game handles information on iOS and Android.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. Specifically, the game does not:

  • ask you to create an account or sign in;
  • ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts or photos;
  • access your location, camera or microphone;
  • contain advertising or advertising identifiers;
  • contain analytics or tracking tools;
  • offer in-app purchases or process payments.

3. Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your own device so you can continue where you left off. This includes the pilot name you choose, coins and gems, unlocked aircraft, level scores and your settings. This information never leaves your device and we cannot see it.

You can erase it at any time with Settings > Reset all progress inside the game, or by uninstalling the game.

4. Motion sensor (tilt controls)

If you turn on tilt controls, the game reads your device's motion sensor to steer the aircraft. On iPhone and iPad you are asked for permission first. The sensor readings are used only while you play, only on your device, and are not recorded or sent anywhere. Tilt controls are optional and are off unless you turn them on.

5. Internet connections

The game works offline. When your device is online, the game downloads its display fonts from Google Fonts. As with any internet request, Google receives technical information such as your device's IP address in order to deliver the font files. We do not receive this information. Google describes how it handles these requests in its Google Fonts privacy information.

The game makes no other internet connections.

6. Sharing of information

Because we do not collect personal information, we have nothing to share, sell or transfer to anyone.

7. Children

The game is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from anyone else.

8. Your choices

  • Delete your saved progress with Settings > Reset all progress, or uninstall the game.
  • Turn tilt controls on or off under Settings > Flight controls, or withdraw motion permission in your device settings.
  • Play with your device offline if you prefer the game to make no internet connections.

9. Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects privacy, we will update this page and change the effective date above before the change reaches players.

10. Contact
